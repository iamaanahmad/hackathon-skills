# Idea Scoring

Score each area from 1-10:
- Originality
- Problem Strength
- Technical Depth
- Sponsor Alignment
- Demo Potential
- UX Potential
- Feasibility
- Judge Appeal
- Grand Prize Potential

Guideline:
- 1-3: weak
- 4-6: viable but unremarkable
- 7-8: competitive
- 9-10: exceptional evidence required
